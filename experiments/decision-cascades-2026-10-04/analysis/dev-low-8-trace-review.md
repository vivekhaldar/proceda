## E1086: genuine classification error before any gate

The input is “Need help with bulk pricing for P23V4W.” The expected intent is `generic question about a listing`, with action `no action`. This is a reasonable label: the seller does not assert that a listed price is incorrect.

Baseline and 4B classify it correctly and make no catalog call. In the 12B arm, the primary LLM misclassifies Step 1 as `concern about incorrect pricing`, before the first decision evaluation. It then fetches price unnecessarily. The Step 2 gate abstains (obligations 0.0639; advance 0.0324); the LLM still reports the incorrect intent, with the correct `no action`. Count this as a real end-to-end regression in that arm, but it is not an accepted-gate error. The trace alone does not establish a causal model-arm effect from this stochastic primary-LLM difference.

## 12B patient gates: missing documentation evidence

Step 5 requires checking pharmacy network participation **and documenting any special handling requirements**. In P100012 and P100015, the accepted tool receipt contains only `{"pharmacy_check":"yes"}`. No requirements or explicit “none” are recorded. The full-obligation acceptance is therefore questionable: absence of a field cannot establish absence of a requirement. This is a missing-evidence finding, not evidence that the pharmacies are ineligible or that a particular special requirement exists. Both benchmark results remain PASS.

P100012’s lifestyle-risk and registration acceptances were otherwise grounded; its registration inputs match the observed valid insurance, low risks, and verified pharmacy results. The two questionable pharmacy cases are saved separately as diagnostic fixtures; their reviewer expectation is not benchmark ground truth.

## Shared score limitation: response templates

The generic email branch asks for a standardized response template. E1086 completion summaries in baseline, 4B, and 12B claim a template was prepared but contain no actual template text or artifact. The case traces linked above show the claim; the output scorer checks only `seller_intent` and `action`. This shared generative-path limitation must not be presented as gate-induced, and matching those two labels should not be described as complete fulfillment of every prose instruction.

## Provenance defect and subsequent fix

The runtime’s previous synthetic completion summary said “Step obligations accepted by the configured decision gate” and included runtime receipt IDs. Later primary-LLM completions imitated it after their own gates had abstained: 13 such summaries across all eight referral 4B cases, plus P100012 Step 4 in the 12B arm. Some reused prior-step IDs; others invented IDs. Structured decision events and immutable receipt records remained correct, and required app tools still ran.

Concrete examples: ACC100001 Step 3 invents `receipt_9d0d0d0d0d0d0d0d0d0d0d0d0d0d0d0d`; P100012 Step 4 repeats the actual Step 3 receipt `receipt_99d6dd420d464cc2923529d540e6ce2c` despite its own gate abstaining.

Sources: [ACC100001 events](/Users/Vivek.Haldar/repos/proceda/experiments/decision-cascades-2026-10-04/runs/dev-low-8--referral_abuse_detection_v1/hybrid-local-low/referral_abuse_detection_v1/ACC100001/events.jsonl) and [P100012 events](/Users/Vivek.Haldar/repos/proceda/experiments/decision-cascades-2026-10-04/runs/dev-low-8--patient_intake/hybrid-local-12b-low/patient_intake/P100012/events.jsonl).

After the batch, the runtime summary was changed to plain text: `Step N: TITLE completed. Tool results are retained for subsequent steps.` Gate authority and receipt links stay in structured events/session metadata, outside this model-visible exemplar. A focused regression verifies the next LLM context has no gate-authority phrase or receipt IDs while authoritative completion events retain their links. The 24 decision-executor tests passed. This fixes the source of the misleading exemplar; it does not prove that arbitrary future LLM summaries cannot hallucinate. The saved dev-low-8 traces and scores were not rewritten, and the summary change has not been measured by this batch.
