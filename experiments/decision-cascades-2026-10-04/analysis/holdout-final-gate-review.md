# Independent holdout gate review

Finalized 2026-10-04 after all 720 paired executions finished. This is a read-only review of **completed** cases in the frozen `holdout-final` schedule. It does not inspect future case inputs, change runtime/configuration, or make model calls. Task-output scores and semantic gate correctness are different questions; matching scored output fields does not establish fulfillment of every prose obligation.

## Review method

The [offline checker](review_holdout_gates.py) selects only hybrid case directories with `terminal.json`. For accepted referral gates it compares the supplied evidence with earlier executed tool events, preserving step identity and the complete tool/arguments/result tuple rather than joining globally by provider tool-call ID. It checks exact current-step SOP text, an untruncated response meeting the configured threshold, current/prior-step attribution, immutable receipt contents, unique receipt IDs, successful/persisted results, matching input account, canonical tool/alias, and the fields explicitly requested by the SOP. Structured automatic-completion events must point to the accepted current-step receipts. It also checks the complete referral tool order and independently calculates the SOP's reported action from already-executed results.

Every saved LLM request in reviewed cases is scanned for the former synthetic gate-authority phrase and runtime receipt IDs. Absence verifies removal of that specific misleading exemplar; it is not a proof that arbitrary model prose cannot hallucinate authority. Qualitative review sampled positive and negative enforcement outcomes and threshold/tie cases, and inspected every scored failure in both arms. No patient gate accepted, so there are no patient acceptances to judge against their prose obligations; this absence must not be described as measured patient acceptance accuracy.

Machine-readable per-case evidence and exact audit paths are recorded in [holdout-final-gate-checks.json](holdout-final-gate-checks.json). These checks are analysis only: their domain-specific score calculation is not used by Proceda, the decision service, or the benchmark inference path.

### Email E1012: intent classification before any gate

Input: “When will my P78X9Y listing go live?” Expected: `concern about their product not being listed` / `share listing status`. Hybrid prediction: `generic question about a listing` / `no action`.

The primary LLM misclassifies the go-live question in Step 1. Following its chosen generic branch, it then performs no catalog retrieval; **no decision model is called at all** in this case. The final XML contains the same wrong intent and action, so the failure is not caused by output extraction. The expected listing-status interpretation is supported by the seller asking when the listing will be live. Raw FAIL and both incorrect field scores are retained. At this review time the baseline counterpart has not run, so no paired claim is made.

### Intermediate arithmetic error despite a correct final score

ACC100008's hybrid Step 4 LLM summary reports scores 3/4/3/**0**. The correct No Violation score is **1**, because `address_validity=true` is one qualifying indicator. Misleading Ad Copy still wins with score 4, so Account Closure and the benchmark PASS remain correct. This is a concrete limitation of judging complete SOP execution solely from the final action field. It is **not an observed gate false acceptance**: the accepted Step 1 receipt includes every requested field with the correct values, including `address_validity=true`. The arithmetic is performed later by the generative model, and the trace does not establish that gate acceptance caused the error.

## Limits

This review checks recorded evidence and observable obligations, not truthfulness of the benchmark's simulated tools or calibration of model probabilities. It cannot measure omitted real-world side effects beyond the saved mock-tool traces. An acceptance can be questionable even when the benchmark's narrower output-field scorer reports PASS.
